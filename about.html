About me

The artist behind the work.

[your name]

illustrator • hobby artist • graphic designer

I was a self-taught artist before I hit ten years of age. I started mostly by understanding the basics of drawing cartoon characters and cartoon-style images. I was also quite a bookworm and spent a lot of time reading science fiction, historical fiction, and fantasy books, sometimes winding up finishing the book in one day. Around fourth grade, I started to learn the violin, and around sixth grade, I started to learn the piano, and I have never let go of either since. On top of that, I dabbled in theatre, especially in high school, where I practiced Shakespearean plays and monologues and played in the pit orchestra for musical theatres. Furthermore, I have been in choir since I was in elementary school and did a couple of talent shows for fun, here and there. But my passions always involved exploring the arts along with my scientific endeavors and I turned that into a practical thing by making logos and flyers for clubs that I either was a part of or for other clubs, and making portraits for friends and family. It was not until college that I took a formal fine arts class and learned the proper techniques of art and started to look into the basic principles of design. On the side I also spend time sketching and watching anime. My favorite shows are JoJo's Bizarre Adventure (I've watched that numerous times), Demon Slayer, and Jujutsu Kaisen. I also take inspiration from the stars and am an avid hobby astronomer who loves looking at the stars and studying exoplanets or new facts about our Solar System at the same time. I aim to improve in my hobby craft every day, inspired by the world around me. I hope that you come along with me on the journey.
